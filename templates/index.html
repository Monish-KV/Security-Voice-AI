        PROTOTYPE BOUNDARY

        What protection means here

        VoiceShield provides a security recommendation such as ALLOW, MONITOR, VERIFY, ESCALATE, or BLOCK. It does not execute payments, access bank accounts, trigger MFA, place calls, or control telecom systems.

        For high-impact situations, follow the recommended trusted callback, secondary authorization, or supervisor approval process outside this prototype.

        INTERPRETATION

        What these numbers mean

        Accuracy measures the fraction of correctly classified samples. Precision measures how often FAKE predictions were actually FAKE. Recall measures how many known FAKE samples were detected. F1 combines precision and recall. False-positive rate measures how often REAL recordings were incorrectly flagged as FAKE. False-negative rate measures how often FAKE recordings were missed.

        These values describe the labeled evaluation dataset currently stored in this prototype. They should not be presented as universal real-world detector performance unless the dataset and evaluation methodology support that claim.
